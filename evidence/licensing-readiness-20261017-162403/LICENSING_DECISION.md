# Trade-context licensing decision

Assessment/access date: **17 October 2026, Asia/Qatar**. Decision: **no provider integration or public redistribution authorized by this assessment**. Clarification request is drafted, not sent. No accounts registered, terms accepted or charges incurred.

## Verified facts and pending interpretation

The owner describes a portfolio/demo maritime risk and supply-chain decision-support platform with potential public application, dashboard and repository access; future commercial use is possible, not approved or established. Exact charging model, record ceiling, caching duration, export scope and public release scope remain decisions, not assumed facts.

The [UN Comtrade redistribution guidance](https://uncomtrade.org/docs/re-dissemination-of-data/) permits limited tables and free visualization, and describes a commercial exception below 100,000 total records. Other sections require licences for paid applications and differ on premium-subscription necessity. Reformatting alone is not substantial transformation. **The unresolved question is which provision controls this project's public demo, cached evidence/repository samples, derived indicators and any later paid service.** A small request is not proof of a small total product. No general commercial prohibition is inferred; no unanswered question is treated as permission.

The [data-use policy](https://uncomtrade.org/docs/policy-on-comtrade-data-use/) distinguishes internal institutional use from external dissemination, requires attribution to UN Comtrade and applies licence terms to free and premium access. It also permits variable quotas and service restrictions. The external/mixed audience does not qualify solely as internal use. [Subscription plans](https://uncomtrade.org/docs/subscriptions/) describe access tiers, not blanket redistribution rights. The applicable tier and effective endpoint limits must be confirmed before scheduling ingestion. [Preview documentation](https://uncomtrade.org/docs/what-is-data-preview/) warns of truncated results and throttling; a public preview is insufficient evidence of complete coverage.

All four official pages above were accessed on 2026-10-17. These are paraphrased findings, not a legal opinion or signed provider permission. Their current live content may change. Prior technical evidence remains in [trade assessment](../trade-qualification-20261017-160824/ASSESSMENT.md) and [sample verification](../trade-qualification-20261017-160824/sample-verification.json): Singapore annual 2025 aggregate exists; August 2026 sample is empty; WTO returned 401. No new source query was required.
